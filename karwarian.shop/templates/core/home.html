About Karwar

Karwar is the district headquarters of Uttara Kannada, nestled between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats. Known for its untouched beaches, naval heritage, and the serene beauty of the Kali River, Karwar is a hidden gem of coastal Karnataka.
